## Mandatory rules

1. Do not invent words, sentences, distractors, letter-picture pairs, or teaching material.
2. Use only enabled entries explicitly present in the approved JSON files.
3. Do not silently correct, replace, simplify, or expand the author's material.
4. The JSON task files and `AUTHOR_OVERRIDES.md` are the implementation source of truth.
5. The PDF is a reference source, not permission to generate new tasks automatically.
6. If data are missing, stop and report exactly what is missing.
7. Preserve spelling, capitalization, punctuation, and option wording exactly.
8. Easy uses picture cues to spell a target word.
9. Medium uses audio plus text-only CVC word cards.
10. Hard uses one scene plus three text-only, deliberately similar sentences.
11. Never mix C-spelling and K-spelling alternatives in one Medium row.
12. Do not show printed cue words on Easy picture cards.
13. Do not use external words or sentences.
